The Maestro's Journey

"Simon Joseph doesn't just teach music; he cultivates artists."

A Legacy of Musical Excellence

Simon Joseph is more than just a musician; he is an institution in music education. With a profound understanding of harmonic theory, rhythm, and instrumental mechanics, he has dedicated his life to the pursuit of musical perfection.

As a highly respected educator at Accord School, Sir Simon has profoundly impacted the school's cultural landscape. He is responsible for nurturing the raw talent of countless students.

Teaching Philosophy & Vision

Sir's teaching philosophy is rooted in the powerful belief that every student possesses a unique musical voice waiting to be discovered. He tailors his lesson plans to fit the individual's pace and ambition.

Beyond technical proficiency, Simon Joseph emphasizes ear training, improvisational skills, and stage presence. His students at Accord School are routinely prepared for school assemblies, and high-stakes talent shows.

Curriculum & Expertise

Master the fundamentals. Command the stage.

Guitar Mastery

Comprehensive training spanning Acoustic, Classical, and Electric guitar. From foundational open chords to complex rock/metal solos.

Keyboard & Piano

Deep-dive instruction into piano mechanics. Students learn sight-reading, scale mastery, chord progressions, and independent hand coordination.

Accord School Support

Exclusive mentorship for Accord School students. Dedicated preparation for school events, practical exams, and live performance coaching.
